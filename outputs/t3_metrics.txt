CONCLUSION:

LLMs ALONE CANNOT BE TRUSTED FOR INCIDENT DATA ACCURACY:
Without verified incident data, both models performed poorly:
  • Groq (no data): Spearman ρ = 0.0953, median error = 75.13%
  • Gemini (no data): Spearman ρ = 0.7027, median error = 42.78%
Neither model's unaided estimates correlated reliably with actual 
precinct safety levels. Even the better-performing Gemini (ρ=0.70) 
fell far short of a trusted recommendation engine.

VERIFIED DATA TRANSFORMS MODEL ACCURACY:
When both models were provided actual incident data, they achieved 
near-perfect accuracy:
  • Groq (with data): Spearman ρ = 0.8475 → 0.0% median error
  • Gemini (with data): Spearman ρ = 0.9165 → 0.0% median error
Both models correctly identified the top 5 highest-incident precincts.
The dramatic improvement shows that LLMs excel at *reasoning over* 
data, not at *remembering* it accurately.

WHY SAFESTAY USES VERIFIED DATA, NOT LLM MEMORY:
SafeStay relies on verified incident data (not LLM estimates) because:
  1. LLMs hallucinate: Without grounding, their incident estimates 
     are unreliable and drift far from reality (75% median error).
  2. Users deserve transparency: Verified data is auditable and 
     documented; LLM estimates are black-box and cannot explain 
     why a neighborhood is marked unsafe.
  3. Data-grounded insights are trustworthy: When an LLM reasons 
     over verified NYPD data, it produces accurate, defensible 
     recommendations (0% error) that users can verify independently.

RECOMMENDATION:
Build SafeStay's safety assessments on verified incident data from 
authoritative sources (NYPD, public health, etc.). Use LLMs to 
synthesize and reason over that data—not to replace it.
